# Arrival sound effects

The four MP3 files in this directory are original Calder Bay edits and mixes. They combine filtered recordings, timing edits, level changes, fades, and generated water noise. Files containing Work With Sounds material are distributed under CC BY 4.0. `crossing.mp3`, `harbor.mp3`, and `landing.mp3` also contain edited material offered under the Pixabay Content License.
